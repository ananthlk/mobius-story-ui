is the right call.

Full control.
100% BHPF-owned. No outside investors at the table. No board complexity. BHPF decides — on governance, on pace, on who joins.

Cash-positive in 12 months.
Break-even by Month 12 on a $3.5M raise. Trough is only $0.99M. From Year 2 the business funds its own growth. Structure B doesn't cash-flow positively for 3+ years.

Florida is a real business.
~80 CMHCs. $38M ARR at saturation. 41% EBITDA margin. ~$8M/yr dividend to BHPF by Year 5. This is not a stepping stone — it is a durable, high-quality asset.

act7-entrants
Act VII · 03 — New Entrants
Exit slots become
entry points.

When an existing owner exits, their equity stake is offered to outside CMHCs — Florida behavioral health centers not currently in the BHPF network.

New entrants come in at a 25% premium to the founding-cohort rate. This premium compensates existing owners for the risk they took at formation.

If demand for the platform is strong enough, a formal additional raise may follow — opening participation to new entrants even without an exit slot.
