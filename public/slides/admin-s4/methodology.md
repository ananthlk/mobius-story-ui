# Methodology — admin-s4

## footprint
$1.1B / 34 / 11,700 / 310K from `org_kpis_v2` aggregates + BHPF directory + member-reported staffing/patient counts.

## metric_evidence
- Calls/FTE — CFO Survey 2025 today vs network-scale projection from AI CoE design (centralized routing model).
- Denial rate — CFO Survey today vs ADS/SimiTree benchmark (achievable post-AI denial engine).
- AR days — CFO Survey today vs MGMA benchmark; $140M freed-cash math: ($1.1B × 50/365 days).
- Admin % — APQC top quartile target (8%) vs current 11.7%; $41M = 3.7pp × $1.1B.

## fn_consolidation
Per-function FTE-reduction targets derived from APQC FTE-per-revenue ratios at $1B+ scale (e.g. IT 2.7% benchmark vs current 3.4%). Savings sum: $7M IT + $4M Call Center + $14M Billing/RCM + $4M HR ≈ $29M (the AI dividend; scale dividend $37M layered on top).

## ai_coe_total
- Total $66M = scale dividend ($37M, FTE consolidation only) + AI dividend ($29M, automation on top of consolidation).
- Excludes revenue recovery (Act IV: rate, leakage, churn) and clinical impact.
