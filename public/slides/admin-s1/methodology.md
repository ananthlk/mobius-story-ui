# Methodology — admin-s1

## fte_vs_pcp
- BHPF FTE per dept: CFO Survey 2025, mid-tier respondents ($15–30M revenue).
- PCP benchmark: MGMA 2024 PCP practice averages.
- Comparison made at same revenue band ($15M reference) to control for scale.

## outcomes
- **AR days (BHPF 44 / MGMA 28)**: Self-reported median across CFO survey respondents; MGMA 28 from MGMA DataDive 2024 multi-specialty median.
- **Calls/FTE/day (BHPF 107 / Benchmark 160)**: CFO survey IT/IS respondents; benchmark from industry data referenced in CFO survey design.
- **Denial rate (BHPF 18% / ADS 5.7%)**: BHPF: CFO Survey 2025 self-report. ADS/SimiTree BH 2026: industry-published benchmark.
- **Turnover (34% / 18%)**: NSI 2024 BH sector for BHPF reference; health-system sector for benchmark.

## Limitations
- All BHPF figures are self-reported; small sample sizes per tier.
- Working-capital and lost-collections estimates ($4–6M, $8–12M) are rough — assumes BHPF-network revenue base and standard collection-cycle math.
