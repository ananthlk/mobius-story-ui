How to read this analysis

About BHPF
Behavioral Health Partners of Florida is a coalition of 25 community mental health centers — the providers carrying the hardest patients on Medicaid rates, in communities others left behind.
⚠ Data scope
CMS DOGE captures fee-for-service claims only. Managed care encounters, non-Medicaid revenue, and grant-funded services are not visible. Every insight is directionally strong — not the full financial picture.
Qualitative layer
Where claims data couldn't answer the question, we went to the source. Conversations with BHPF member CMHCs helped validate what the numbers suggested — and surface what they couldn't show.
Data foundation
Source
CMS DOGE — Medicaid fee-for-service claims, Florida only, 2018–2024. The full FL behavioral health market (~$1.1B baseline in 2019).
81 procedure codes
Filtered to BH codes reimbursed under the FL Medicaid fee schedule. Excludes per-diem residential and medical crossover claims.
Org classification
Segments — CMHC, FQHC, Specialty BH, Ind. BH, SUD — derived from NPPES taxonomy, FL licensure, and Medicaid enrollment records.
⚠ 2024 note
2024 covers 12 months. A small share of late claims may not yet be adjudicated. Use directionally.
What each metric means
Market share
Segment's share of total FL Medicaid BH revenue. Numerator and denominator from the same claims universe.
Revenue per beneficiary (RPB)
Total paid ÷ unique benes. Captures rate and utilization together. Shown as $ + % vs FL market average.
Service mix ratio
Ongoing community care ÷ front-end care (intake, crisis, ACT). Higher = more sustained treatment relative to acute episodes.
Panel commitment
Share of clinicians billing Medicaid in month M who bill again in M+1. Medicaid panel continuity — not employment retention.
Reading the signals
Green / above market
Above the FL volume-weighted average. Not automatically good — a high RPB on a small panel may reflect selective intake, not higher rates.
Red / below market
Below FL average. Often structural — orgs with broad, lower-acuity panels will show lower RPB even when running efficiently.
The market average
Volume-weighted across all FL BH orgs. Large orgs with flat rates pull it down. A floor — not a target.
Ask anything
Every metric node is clickable. Opens a live AI analyst pre-loaded with that data. Plain language — no SQL required.
