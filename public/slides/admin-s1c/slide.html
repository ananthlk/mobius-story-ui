Why the 2:1 ROI pitch never lands
Fixed costs don't scale down
Sales + integration = $150K per deal regardless of org size. At $25M revenue that's 50% of deal value — before you see a dollar of ROI.
The product wasn't built for you
Roadmaps are funded by $500M health systems. You get the same product at nearly the same price — with a fraction of the features you actually need.
Implementation is a fixed tax
18–24 months. Staff distraction. Change management. The same overhead whether you're $30M or $300M — but it hits harder when your margin is thin.
