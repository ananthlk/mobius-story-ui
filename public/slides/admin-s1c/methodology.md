# Methodology — admin-s1c

## roi_curve
Per-tier deal economics computed as:
- **Deal value** ≈ org revenue × ~2.8% (industry avg per-org SaaS spend on EHR/RCM).
- **Fixed cost** = $150K (sales + integration; from cross-vendor deck math + Waystar 10-Q margin analysis).
- **Vendor margin** = curve from 12% at $10M tier to 55% at $1.5B tier (reflects pricing-power and operational efficiency at scale).
- **Client ROI** = (deal value − fixed cost − vendor margin) / fixed cost.

Dots placed on log-scale x-axis ($10M to $1.5B); break-even at y=0 shaded red.

## Limitations
- 2.8% revenue→deal-value heuristic is an industry average, not deal-specific.
- ROI multiples are illustrative, not audited per-deal P&Ls.
- Vendor margin curve is a simplification — actual vendor economics vary by product line.
